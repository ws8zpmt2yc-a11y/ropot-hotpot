How RoPot works

Tell us what you want.
Our arms pick it fresh.
You cook it your way.

  1. 1

    You choose

    Pick your broth, set the spice, then tap the ingredients you want from the ring. Twenty-four trays, no menu-reading.

  2. 2

    They pick

    Two robotic arms turn, reach into the tray you chose and portion it straight into your bowl. You watch the whole thing happen.

  3. 3

    You enjoy

    The kitchen ladles the broth, boxes it up and texts you. Collect, get home, cook it exactly how you like it.

To be clear about who does what: the arms select and portion ingredients, and our service robots run the floor. The cooking is done by people — a kitchen team out the back, and a manager who has never once been a robot.

Meet RoPot

Robots on the floor.
People on the wok.

Almost everyone you meet at RoPot is a robot. The servers bring your trays, and two arms on the ingredient station pick every portion the moment you order it. It makes the whole thing quicker, more precise and — we’ll admit it — more fun to watch.

What the robots don’t do is cook. The broths, the dumplings and every bit of knife work belong to the kitchen team out the back, and there’s a manager on the floor for anything that needs a person.

The serversRobot
They carry trays to your table, clear plates and never forget which pot was yours. Say hello — they like it.
The picking armsRobot
Two collaborative arms on the ingredient station. They select and portion, precisely, the second you order.
The kitchenHuman
Broths, dumplings, knife work, seasoning. Every bit of cooking is done by people out the back.
The managerHuman
Runs the floor, sorts your booking, and is the one to talk to when something isn't right.
